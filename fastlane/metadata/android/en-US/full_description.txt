PhoneZen replaces your default phone application with a smarter, privacy-respecting experience.

Key Features:
• Advanced Anti-Spam: Built-in call blocker following ARCEP rules (Naegelen Law) and community-driven spam identification.
• Driving Mode: Automatic SMS response and call handling while driving to keep you safe on the road.
• Rich Call History: Clean, detailed, and searchable call log layout.
• Privacy First: 100% free and open-source software. No proprietary dependencies, no Google Play Services, and no Firebase tracking.
